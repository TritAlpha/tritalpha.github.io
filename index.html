About Me

I am a PhD student in medicinal chemistry at the School of Pharmacy, Yantai University.

My research focuses on computational approaches for natural product drug discovery, particularly the application of deep learning to triterpenoid molecular generation and biological activity prediction.

I am interested in developing molecular representations, generative models, and predictive models that can better capture the complex polycyclic scaffolds and stereochemical characteristics of triterpenoids.

Research Interests

Deep Learning for Drug Discovery

Application of modern machine learning and deep learning methods to molecular design and drug discovery.

Triterpenoid Molecular Generation

Development of generative models for exploring triterpenoid chemical space while preserving complex polycyclic scaffolds.

Biological Activity Prediction

Predictive modeling of compound activity using molecular representations and limited experimental datasets.

Natural Product Drug Discovery

Computational exploration and optimization of natural products and natural-product-derived compounds.
